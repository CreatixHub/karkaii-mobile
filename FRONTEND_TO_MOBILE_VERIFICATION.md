> The earlier statuses in this file said "Verified" for all 59 pages, but no field-by-field comparison against the frontend source
> existed. They have been reset. The mobile module screens are being **rebuilt on a shared engine** (`src/features/engine`) with a

>
> | Status | Meaning |
> |---|---|
> | **Implemented (engine)** | Rebuilt on the new engine with real local CRUD, persistence, role gating. Bundles for Android + iOS; seed data and business logic covered by Node tests (1,028 checks across Finance, HRM, Academic, People admin, Students, Teachers, Campus Operations, Reports, Communication and Administration). **Not yet run on a device/emulator.** |
> | **Legacy – unverified** | Old monolithic screen (`src/app/(app)/<section>.tsx`), data resets on restart, not role-gated, not compared to the frontend. Queued for rebuild. |
>
> Seed dates: relative data (attendance, leave, homework, expenses) is anchored to the day the data is first created; calendar-year data (holiday calendar, fee due dates) is fixed. Per-day registers open on the latest day that has records.
>
> Currency: the frontend formats money as `$` (en-US); mobile matches (`src/core/utils/format.ts`).
